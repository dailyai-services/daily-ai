## How a project starts

1. **Tell us what you need.** Share your business or idea and what the website should help visitors do.
2. **Clarify the scope.** We discuss pages, content, features, timeline, budget and any dependencies.
3. **Receive a proposal.** We confirm deliverables, exclusions, milestones, revisions and payment terms in writing.
4. **Build after agreement.** Work begins after the scope and initial payment milestone are agreed.

Domain registration, hosting, paid themes, stock assets and third-party service charges are not included unless explicitly agreed. Any such third-party expense is confirmed with the client before purchase and paid by the client.
